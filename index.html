Cancer

Cancer is one of the leading cause for death in this world having nearly 626,140 cancer deaths just in 2026 according to The American Cancer Society's Cancer Statistics

Cancer begins when normal cells acquire mutations in their DNA that disrupt the usual controls over cell growth, division, and death. Normally, cells follow a strict cycle: they grow, divide to replace old or damaged cells, perform their specialized functions, and die through a process called apoptosis. When this balance is disturbed, cells may divide uncontrollably, ignore signals to stop growing, and avoid programmed cell death.These abnormal cells can accumulate and form tumors, which may be benign (non-cancerous) or malignant (cancerous).

Cancer has different types:

Carcinoma

Sarcoma

Lymphoma

Gram Cell Tumor

Blastoma
